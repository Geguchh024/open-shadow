# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release:
  - Shadow lights: a Spot, Area, Sun or Point lamp with a hidden pattern
    plane that only casts shadows, for Cycles and EEVEE.
  - 25 procedural patterns: Window, Blinds, Grid / Lattice, Honeycomb,
    Moroccan, Rose Window, Gothic Window, Iron Gate, Pipes, Leaves, Fern,
    Palm, Blades, Bamboo, Branches, Pine, Hanging Vines, Cacti, Cobweb,
    Spider Web, Broken Glass, Barbed Wire, Curtain, Caustics and Breakup,
    plus your own image.
  - 60 presets with a thumbnail gallery.
  - Coloured light through stained glass (Cycles).
  - Placement (focus distance, size, scale, rotation, offset) and finishing
    (strength, invert, contrast, tint, iris) for every pattern.
  - Optional world haze for visible light beams.
  - Python-free drivers, so files render without the add-on.
